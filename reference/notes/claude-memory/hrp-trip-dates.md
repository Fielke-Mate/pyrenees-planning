**Status (2026-10-03): this trip is in the past.** The project has since become a
Pyrenees-wide planner (`site/planner.html`, plan and open questions in `site/ROADMAP.md`);
the original single-trip tool still lives at the site root pending Martin's decision on
whether to archive it. Treat the dates below as history, not as an upcoming deadline.

The HRP Pont d'Espagne → Luchon trip (planned in `hrp_wallon_luchon.html`) is framed by a
**Friday afternoon departure from Munich**, arriving Lourdes Saturday morning, so **hiking
starts on the Saturday**.

For 2026 that fixes the dates as:
- Depart Munich **Fri 11 Sep**, 14:20 (ICE + TGV iNOUI, overnight via Paris → Toulouse)
- Arrive **Lourdes Sat 12 Sep 08:58**
- Walk **Sat 12 → Thu 17 Sep** (6 days, D1–D6)
- Return from Luchon **Fri 18 Sep 06:40**

**Why:** Martin has stated the Friday-afternoon Munich departure more than once. I twice
mislabelled the itinerary by anchoring on the wrong axis — first leaving the dates alone and
shifting every day *name* forward (making Day 1 a Sunday), which is exactly backwards.

**How to apply:** The **day names are the fixed reference** (Sat start), not the dates. If a
day/date pair looks inconsistent, correct the date, not the name. Day-of-week matters
materially here because bus 965 Lourdes→Cauterets runs a reduced weekend timetable, so getting
it wrong changes the whole arrival analysis. Verify any date/day pair against the real calendar
before editing. Related: [[verify-map-data-never-guess]]
